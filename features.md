E.g. “Valley 6 / 9 °C · Fell-tops -2 / 2 °C, wind +20–30 mph” so
 people can relate forecast to where they’ll actually be.

Add freezing level / snow line to the main cards

A single line under each day: “Freezing: 600 m” or “Snow line:
 500–700 m” is extremely useful for route choice and kit.

Highlight “worst case” days directly on the cards

Small tags like “Windiest”, “Wettest”, “Coldest” so duty leaders
 can scan quickly without reading every number.

Show wind chill for today and the windiest day

One extra field: “Wind chill on tops: -10 °C” helps with PPE and
 exposure decisions.

Make the risk colours more obvious on the 7-day strip

Add a thin colour bar or dot on each day card (Green/Amber/Red) for
 overall risk so you see the pattern across the week at a glance.

Turn “Today – detail” into a simple hourly or time-of-day split

Even just “Daytime / Evening / Overnight” with wind and temp helps
 for night exercises and typical call-out times.

 for Sat 00:00–23:59” to avoid confusion when forecasts are stale.
